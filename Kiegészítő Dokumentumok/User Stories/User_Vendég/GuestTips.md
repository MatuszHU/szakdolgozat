# Guest Tips

**Requirements:** M11, M8
**Feature file:** [`Nightlife/NightlifeTests/Features/GuestTips.feature`](../../../Nightlife/NightlifeTests/Features/GuestTips.feature)

## User Story
As a guest
I want short pop-up tips with pictures where I need them
So that I understand the app without reading a manual

## Acceptance criteria
The acceptance criteria are the scenarios of the feature file above (single source of truth):

* The first visit of the home screen, the ticket shop, my tickets, the map and the raffles shows a tip with a picture, a title and a short text
* A tip that the guest has read ("Got it") is not shown again, not even after a restart
* Only one tip is shown at a time
* The tips can be shown again from the settings (M8)
