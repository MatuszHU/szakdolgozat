# Guest Settings

**Requirements:** M8, M6
**Feature file:** [`Nightlife/NightlifeTests/Features/GuestSettings.feature`](../../../Nightlife/NightlifeTests/Features/GuestSettings.feature)

## User Story
As a guest
I want to reach every setting available to me in one view
So that I do not have to look for them

## Acceptance criteria
The acceptance criteria are the scenarios of the feature file above (single source of truth):

* The settings offer the profile picture, the about section and signing out
* The about section shows who is signed in and the version of the app
* Signing out from the settings returns to the welcome screen (M6)
